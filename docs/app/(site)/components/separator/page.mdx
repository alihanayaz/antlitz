## Vertical

<Preview file="separator" name="Vertical" />

A vertical separator needs a parent with a resolved height — it is `h-full`, so
it collapses inside a container that sizes to its content.

## Decorative

<Preview file="separator" name="Decorative" layout="col" />

`decorative` sets `role="none"` and `aria-hidden`, removing the rule from the
accessibility tree. Use it when the line is purely visual and the sections
either side are already distinguished by headings or landmarks.

Leave it off when the rule is the only thing separating two groups of content,
where its implicit `separator` role carries meaning.
